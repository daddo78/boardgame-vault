# What's new in Board Game Curator

Release notes for testers. Newest first. Post one to Discord with `node tools/post-update.js v0.11`.

## v0.12 · October 20, 2026

**Fixes**
- Asking about a game you don’t own finds that exact game ("Horror on the Orient Express" no longer turns into "Dead Man on the Orient Express").
- Nathan’s "worth a look" tip never suggests a game you already own.

## v0.11 · October 20, 2026

**Your profile and wishlist**
- **Top 10 games of all time:** pick and order yours in My profile. Any game counts, owned or not.
- **Profile pages:** open a friend's from Friends → Profile to see their taste, Top 10, most played games and wishlist.
- **Wishlists 🎁:** save any game Nathan suggests, or one from a friend's library, with "Add to my wishlist". Friends can see each other's wishlists (gift ideas!). When you get one, tap "Got it!" and it moves onto your shelf.

**Nathan got social**
- "What can we play at Nathan's tonight?" picks from that friend's shelf.
- "Who has a good co-op for 2?" looks across all your friends' shelves and says whose copy to borrow.
- "Which of my friends would love Brass?" matches a game to your friends' tastes.

**Rules**
- Every game has a **Rulebook** section. Add the PDF once (most publishers post theirs free) and everyone can open it.
- Ask Nathan "How do I set up Brass?" and he answers from the rulebook. If there's no rulebook yet, he answers from memory, says so, and points you to the official one on BoardGameGeek.

**Nathan understands you better**
- Partial or misspelled names work: "tanares adventure" finds Arena: The Contest – Tanares Adventures.
- Describe a game without naming it: "that Viking game by Uwe Rosenberg" gets "Sounds like A Feast for Odin!"
- "A game I don't own" gives ideas to buy, even as a follow-up.
- While he thinks, he tells you what he's up to: "Flipping to the rulebook…", "Browsing the game store aisles…", "Peeking at your friends' shelves…"

**Fixes**
- Signing back in after your sign-in expired no longer leaves the page stuck on "Offline".
- Anyone you've logged even one play with can now be @-mentioned (it used to take 3).

*Behind the scenes: admin tools, a usage dashboard, and nightly automatic checks that Nathan and the site still work.*

## v0.10 · October 19–20, 2026

**Your shelf is home**
- When you sign in you land on your own shelf. To browse a friend's collection, open Friends → View library. You need to be connected by friend code first.
- While you're on someone else's shelf, the top shows "Dan's shelf · view only" with a Close button.

**One set of stars**
- The stars on a game are just your rating. Tap them on your own shelf to rate.

**Nathan knows a lot more**
- Ask about any game, even ones you don't own: "What genre is Terraforming Mars?", "Is Cascadia good for two?"
- "Would I like it?" compares the game to what you play and rate most.
- "What should I buy next?" suggests games you don't own, and mentions when a friend has a copy you could try first.
- Ask for your top ten and he names all ten.
- Follow-ups work: after a top ten, "Which of those are co-op?" stays within those ten.
- "My" means yours: ask about your games while you're looking at a friend's shelf and he answers about your shelf.
- Ask him about himself ("What do you like to play?") and he answers in his own words.

**Adding a big collection**
- Add games → Spreadsheet takes a CSV file. Only the game name is needed, and there's a template to download.
- Tip: export your BoardGameGeek collection, delete what you no longer own, and upload it.
- Search by name lets you tick a game and its expansions and add them together.

## v0.9 · October 19, 2026

**Friends**
- Connect by friend code: enter a friend's code and you're connected right away.
- @mention a friend from any shelf and Nathan knows their profile and play history.

**Nathan**
- He knows who's asking, and talks to you as "you".
- A small counter under the Ask bar shows how many of your 30 daily questions are left. It resets at midnight Eastern.
- "My top ten" now counts all your plays properly.

**On your phone**
- The keyboard goes away after you send a question.
- "Chat more" doesn't pop the keyboard up.
- The + button no longer covers the reply box.

**Adding games**
- BoardGameGeek now brings in your plays only, not your games. Your shelf is what you actually own, and your plays show up on each game as you add it.
- There's always a "Sign in with Google" button, even when the automatic sign-in doesn't appear.

## v0.8 · October 18, 2026

- Board Game Curator moved to its own database, ready for friends.
- Visitors see a welcome page. The collection, people and Nathan are for members.
- My profile: tell Nathan what you like and don't like, and how you like to play.
- Ratings: your stars are your own; BoardGameGeek ratings are shown separately.
- Friends get their own shelves.
- Tip jar ☕ in the top bar.
