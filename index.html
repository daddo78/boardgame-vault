Search BoardGameGeek and pick the right edition. Expansions of games you own are added to that game.

Snap one box or a whole shelf section. Every title it can read is checked against your collection first: games you already own are skipped, expansions of games you own are added to that game, and only new games are created (then looked up on BoardGameGeek).

Got a big collection? Upload a spreadsheet (saved as CSV) with one game per row. Each game is matched on BoardGameGeek and added to your shelf; anything it can’t find is listed for you to add by name.
